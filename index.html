When does The Grand open?

The Grand is planned to open in early 2027. Enquiries for 2027 events are now welcome.

What kinds of events can I host?

The venue is being designed for weddings, business events, celebrations of life, parties, milestones, graduations and school formals.

Can I tour the venue?

Register your interest now and the events team will share tour opportunities as the venue approaches completion.

Is parking available?

Yes. Free parking is available at DOOLEYS Lidcombe, with convenient access from major transport links.
